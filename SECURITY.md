# Security Policy

## Supported versions

| Version | Supported |
|---------|-----------|
| 0.1.x   | Yes       |

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email security reports to the maintainers listed in the repository organization profile. Include reproduction steps, impact assessment, and affected tenant scope if known.

Do not open public issues for undisclosed vulnerabilities.

## Response target

We aim to acknowledge reports within 2 business days and provide a remediation timeline within 10 business days for confirmed issues.
